# Evidence Matrix

| O.A.S.I.S. component | Defensible evidence connection | What must not be claimed |
|---|---|---|
| CDSS architecture | Mazo et al. reviewed breast-cancer CDSS and their clinical functions | That the review validates O.A.S.I.S. clinically |
| Cervical model comparison | Al Mudawi & Alazeb compared LR, SVM, KNN, DT, RF and boosting methods | That their reported accuracy transfers to our deployment |
| Breast multi-feature modelling | Hussain et al. reviewed imaging and non-imaging risk features and AI models | That O.A.S.I.S. implements an imaging model |
| Mammography risk research | Yala et al. developed a mammography-based deep-learning risk model | That a history-only module reproduces Yala's model |
| Population-screening eligibility | India's NCD programme targets people aged 30+ for oral, breast and cervical screening | That every state/facility follows identical operational intervals/tests |
| Cervical screening interval | WHO recommends high-performance cervical screening beginning at 30, with distinct HIV guidance | That WHO guidance alone defines every local pathway |
